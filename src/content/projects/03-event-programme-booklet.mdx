## Project Overview & Blueprint

Folded booklets (such as church service sheets, school play programmes, wedding schedules, or choral concert programmes) are a classic Publisher staple. Many users believe Word cannot create folded booklets and resort to awkward manual two-column hacks.

Word has an exceptional built-in **Book Fold** engine. You type your content naturally across 4 sequential pages, and Word automatically rearranges and pairs the pages for physical two-sided printing.

  title="Booklet Imposition: Publisher vs Word"

## Step 6: Print Duplex (Flip on Short Edge)

This is the critical step where many people accidentally print the inside spread upside down!

<WhereToFindIt
  title="Where to Find Duplex Print Settings"
  ribbonPath={["File", "Print", "Print on Both Sides (Short Edge)"]}
  altRibbonPath={["Printer Properties", "Finishing", "Short Edge"]}
/>

<GotchaBox title="Common Snag — Upside Down Inside Spread" collapsible={false}>
  <p>
    <strong>Why it happens:</strong> Standard multi-page documents flip along the long edge (like a normal bound book). Booklets printed on Landscape A4 sheets must flip along the SHORT edge (the 210 mm vertical edge).
  </p>
  <p>
    <strong>Immediate fix:</strong> In the Print dialogue (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>), change 'Print on Both Sides: Flip pages on long edge' to <strong>Print on Both Sides: Flip pages on short edge</strong>.
  </p>
</GotchaBox>

<MacNote summary="Booklet printing on Word for Mac">
  <p>
    On macOS, open the Print dialogue (<strong>Cmd ⌘ + P</strong>), change the third dropdown from 'Copies & Pages' to <strong>Layout</strong>, and select <strong>Two-Sided: Short-Edge binding</strong>.
  </p>
</MacNote>

## Check Your Work: Booklet Checklist

- **Book Fold Configured:** Page Setup shows 'Multiple pages: Book fold' on A4 Landscape paper.
- **4-Page Rule Met:** The status bar reads exactly **Page 1 of 4** (Booklets must always be multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, etc.).
- **Gutter Breathing Room:** There is a visible 0.5 cm gutter cushion along the inside binding edge of both spreads.
- **Schedule Table Alignment:** The programme schedule on Page 3 fits cleanly on the single page without spilling over.
- **Print Orientation Checked:** Two-sided printing is set to **Flip pages on Short Edge**.
