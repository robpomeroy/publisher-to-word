## Project Overview & Blueprint

Newsletters are where Publisher users most frequently get tangled in Word. In Publisher, you created columns by drawing separate text boxes and pouring text between them. In Word, if you click "Columns → Two", your entire document suddenly splits into two columns—including your masthead!

In this project, you will build a sophisticated **2-Page Club Newsletter** for the *Avon Gardening Society*. You will master the secret weapon of Word page layout: **Continuous Section Breaks**.

## Step 2: Insert the Continuous Section Break

This is the pivotal technique in Word publishing. A **Continuous Section Break** splits the page layout rules into two distinct zones without starting a new page.

<WhereToFindIt
  title="Where to Find Continuous Section Break"
  ribbonPath={["Layout", "Breaks", "Continuous"]}
  altRibbonPath={["Layout", "Page Setup", "Breaks"]}
/>

<StepList
  steps={[
    {
      title: "Place cursor below the separator rule",
      detail: "Click on the blank line directly below your masthead separator line.",
      ribbonBadge: "Cursor Position"
    },
    {
      title: "Insert Continuous Section Break",
      detail: "Go to Layout → Breaks. Under the 'Section Breaks' heading, click 'Continuous'.",
      ribbonBadge: "Layout → Breaks"
    },
    {
      title: "Confirm break with Show/Hide ¶",
      detail: "Press Ctrl+Shift+8 (or Home → Show/Hide ¶). You will see a double dotted line reading 'Section Break (Continuous)' across the page. Your cursor is now in Section 2.",
      ribbonBadge: "Ctrl + Shift + 8"
    }
  ]}
/>

<GotchaBox title="Common Snag — Accidental Section Break Deletion" collapsible={false}>
  <p>
    <strong>Why it happens:</strong> When you press Backspace or Delete on the paragraph mark adjacent to a continuous section break, Word deletes the break. Deleting a break instantly merges Section 2 back into Section 1's single-column settings.
  </p>
  <p>
    <strong>Immediate fix:</strong> Immediately press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to undo. Always keep Show/Hide ¶ enabled so you never accidentally erase a Section Break line.
  </p>
</GotchaBox>

## Check Your Work: Newsletter Checklist

- **Section Continuity:** The masthead spans 1 full-width column, followed immediately by 2 equal-width columns on Page 1.
- **Break Visibility:** Show/Hide ¶ clearly displays 'Section Break (Continuous)' directly between masthead and columns.
- **Linked Text Overflow:** Adding text into the Page 1 sidebar box pushes extra sentences smoothly into the Page 2 continuation box.
- **First Page Unlinked:** Page 1 has NO running header; Page 2 displays the header and 'Page 2'.
- **Two-Page Cap:** Ensure your document is exactly 2 pages long, without an accidental blank 3rd page.
