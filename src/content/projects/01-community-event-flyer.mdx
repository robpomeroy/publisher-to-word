## Project Overview & Blueprint

Single-page flyers are the classic document that sends former Microsoft Publisher users into despair with Word. When you drop in a hero photo, text jumps wildly; when you print, the bottom border vanishes into thin air.

In this mini-project, you will build a professional **Community Summer Fair Flyer** from a blank document. You will see how Word's anchoring, wrapping, and table features let you achieve the exact visual polish of Publisher—without the layout fighting you.

<GotchaBox title="Common Snag — Cut-Off Bottom Border Line" collapsible={false}>
  <p>
    <strong>Why it happens:</strong> Inkjet and laser printers have a physical gripper bar at the bottom that cannot receive toner or ink. Measuring page borders from the 'Edge of page' puts the bottom line inside this unprintable margin.
  </p>
  <p>
    <strong>Immediate fix:</strong> Always set 'Measure from' to 'Text' with a 10 pt offset. This pulls the border safely inside your desktop printer's active printable boundary.
  </p>
</GotchaBox>

## Check Your Work: Flyer Checklist

Before printing or submitting your flyer, verify each of these quality checkpoints:

- [ ] **Page Border Visibility:** The bottom border line is fully visible in Print Preview (`Ctrl+P`) without truncation.
- [ ] **Single Page Integrity:** Look at the status bar at bottom left; verify it reads **Page 1 of 1** (no blank trailing page 2).
- [ ] **Photo Wrap:** The hero photo has a clean white margin around its right and bottom edges, with no text overlapping the picture pixels.
- [ ] **Table Alignment:** The event details table has no visible borders, and the detail descriptions align crisply on the left.
- [ ] **Contact Footer:** Footer text is legible and centered with at least 0.5 cm padding above the bottom border.
