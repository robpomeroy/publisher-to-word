## The Case of the Disappearing Tabs

In Microsoft Publisher, all your layout tools stayed in predictable toolbars or side panels, regardless of what was clicked on the screen.

In Microsoft Word, tools play hide-and-seek. When you click on a picture, a brand-new tab appears on the Ribbon labelled <span class="badge badge-contextual">Picture Format</span>. When you click on a text box, it says <span class="badge badge-contextual">Shape Format</span>. But the moment you click away into a blank paragraph, **that tab vanishes instantly!**

This dynamic behaviour causes endless frustration for beginners. In Word, layout tools live in three distinct places.

<GotchaBox title="Common Snag — 'I clicked Format Shape, but where are text box margins?'" collapsible={false}>
  <p>
    When the Format Shape side pane opens, it defaults to <strong>Shape Options</strong> (which controls the box background colour and line borders). To find internal text margins, you must click <strong>Text Options</strong> at the top of that pane, then click the third little icon (<strong>Text Box</strong>). We will practise this step in Lesson 6!
  </p>
</GotchaBox>

<MacNote summary="Using Word on a Mac? Contextual tabs and floating arch differences">
  <p>
    On Word for Mac, contextual tabs appear highlighted in colour at the end of the ribbon just like on Windows. However, the floating Layout arch icon is occasionally omitted in older Mac Word versions; on Mac, right-clicking the picture and selecting <strong>Wrap Text</strong> is the most dependable shortcut.
  </p>
</MacNote>
