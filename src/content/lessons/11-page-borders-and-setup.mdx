## The Tale of the Missing Bottom Border

In Microsoft Publisher, page setup was straightforward. You picked A4 or Letter, set your margins, and drew a decorative border around the page knowing your printer would reproduce it accurately.

In Microsoft Word, thousands of users run into a classic printer defect: they add a decorative page border, send the flyer to their home or office printer, and **the bottom border line is completely missing!**

This occurs because of a single hidden dropdown setting in Word's Page Borders menu: **Measure from: Edge of page** versus **Measure from: Text**.

<GotchaBox title="Common Snag — 'Where did Page Borders go?'" collapsible={false}>
  <p>
    In older versions of Word, Page Borders lived on the Page Layout tab. In modern Microsoft 365, Word moved Page Borders onto the <strong>Design</strong> tab (look at the far right of the top ribbon).
  </p>
</GotchaBox>

<MacNote summary="Using Word on a Mac? Page borders and printer margins">
  <p>
    On macOS, page borders are located under <strong>Format</strong> menu (at the top of your Mac screen) → <strong>Borders and Shading</strong> → <strong>Page Border</strong> tab, or via the <strong>Design</strong> ribbon tab. Click <strong>Options</strong> to adjust the margin calculation source.
  </p>
</MacNote>
